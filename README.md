# RRGBS JOBS - Android Application

RRGBS (RR Group of Business Solutions) Android mobile application built with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose (Material 3)**.

## Key Portals & Features

1. **AI Resume Builder**
   - Interactive profile editor with multi-section tabs (Personal, Summary, Work History, Education, Skills & Certifications).
   - Real-time ATS compatibility scoring engine (0–100) with detailed parsability feedback.
   - AI action buttons: AI Summary Generator, AI Impact Bullet Enhancer, and keyword optimizer.
   - 4 Professional Templates: Classic, Modern, Executive, and ATS Clean.
   - Live formatted resume preview and export/sharing via Android share intents.

2. **Jobs Portal**
   - Search by role, skills, keywords, and job locations.
   - Category filtering (IT & Software, BPO, Sales, Manufacturing, Logistics, HR, Freshers, Work From Home).
   - Bookmark and saved jobs list.
   - Comprehensive job detail dialog with responsibilities, qualifications, and benefits.
   - Full "Apply Now" application submission flow.
   - Employer "Post Vacancy" modal to dynamically publish new openings.

3. **RRGBS Business Store**
   - Corporate supplies catalog across 8 categories: Housekeeping, Uniforms & Workwear, IT & Laptops, Biometric & Security, Corporate Gifts, Joining Kits, Safety Products, and Office Supplies.
   - Employee Joining Kit onboarding showcase with component breakdown.
   - Cart management with dynamic quantity updates and total estimation.
   - Request Quotation & Order form with corporate company details.

4. **Staffing & Corporate Services**
   - Permanent Hiring, Contract Staffing, RPO Services, Payroll Outsourcing, Bulk Hiring, and Facility & Manpower Support.
   - Service breakdown and direct mandate proposal inquiry form.
   - Statutory compliance and turnaround guarantees.

5. **Doorstep Home Services**
   - Home Cleaning & Housekeeping, Elderly Care & Support, Home Nursing, Home Maintenance, and Security/Caretaker services.
   - Interactive Instant Cost Estimator tool calculating estimates based on property size/shift.
   - Direct doorstep service booking form.

6. **Branding & Support**
   - Unified navigation with responsive bottom bar and top app bar.
   - Shivamogga & Bangalore office branches, direct dialer action, and WhatsApp support.
